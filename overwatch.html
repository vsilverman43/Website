About my Career

I started playing shortly after the game release in 2016. I wasn't that great, and started on Xbox. I didn't know what to do and normally playing DPS. I heard some people saying that they "main" someone. So, I just thought to myself that I also needed one. To be apart of the community, so I looked at the box art and it was Tracer. I was like, I am a tracer main now. Not even realizing how high of a ceiling Tracer has to be actually skilled in. Speed up until I started college at SIUc. I did a local tourny at the student center. Not knowing that there was a few people starting a club there for eSports. I was apart of a few that was invited to be apart of their Overwatch team. Even if I wasn't all that great. They saw potential in me and I got a spot. Once we were doing some practicing, they moved me to support, which we were in dying need for at the time. That time, I picked up Moria and that's where my best role was from the three roles that you can choose from. During 2019 and the start of 2020, we played in a national tourny called Tespa and placed in 5th of the states. Another team saw me and invited me to a tryout of their team. I was still a support player and there on out. Around 2022, I was a captain and playing Lucio and Brig. Which is more of an important heroes to have due to their kit. At the end of 2023, I have retired and making about 2k in prize money. Which isn't much, but people around you saying that video games are not valuable. We tried out for Open Devision before OWL dispanded. We didn't make it that far... It was a very great experience to have and nice to have met the people that I did. A video game that brings people together
